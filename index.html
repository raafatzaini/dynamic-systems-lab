Know the building blocks
    
Stock
An amount that builds up or drains away over time, like water in a tank. In the scenes: a tank.
Flow
A rate that fills or drains a stock, per unit of time. In the scenes: a pipe with a valve.
Reinforcing loop (R)
A change that feeds itself: more leads to more (or less to less). In the scenes: an orange link that opens a valve wider as the stock grows.
Balancing loop (B)
A change that pushes back toward a level or goal. In the scenes: a purple link that slows a flow as the stock changes.
Delay
The time it takes for a change to affect a rate. Marked ‖ in the loop diagrams.
